Who we are

ECOSWAP is a waste collection, recycling and sustainable-product business. We gather recyclable materials from individuals, households, schools, businesses and communities, then sort, process and transform them into reusable and valuable products.

Our mission

To reduce environmental pollution by collecting and transforming recyclable waste into useful products, while creating real economic opportunities for individuals and communities.

Our vision

To build a cleaner, circular and sustainable environment where waste is treated as a valuable resource rather than a burden.

/ rewards program

Coins and Diamonds

Coins

Earned on materials weighed in grams. Perfect for everyday drop-offs like bottles, plates and packaging.

Diamonds

Earned on bulk materials weighed in kilograms. Diamonds unlock the higher-value items in our reward catalogue.

Conversion rates and redemption rules are configured by ECOSWAP administrators and can be reviewed at any time — nothing is hard-coded into the website.
